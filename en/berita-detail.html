ONSA Medicine Gold Medal & Etanider English — Al Mazaya SHS

Achievement · Sep 6, 2024

Achievement Sep 6, 2024

Al Mazaya SHS once again made its mark nationally. Windy Zhafira Maheswari won the gold medal at the ONSA Medicine Competition, while Sylla Almira Syarif took 1st place at the provincial-level Etanider English Competition.

This achievement comes from consistent science-olympiad and English coaching — advanced classes, regular try-outs, and intensive mentoring ahead of competitions.

The school congratulates the students, parents, and coaching team. May this inspire all students to compete bravely and bring honor to the school.
