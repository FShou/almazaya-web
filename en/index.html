Four Core Pillars

Education at Al Mazaya is built on four complementary pillars: Islamic Studies, Character Building, Academic Excellence, and English Development.

Islamic Studies

Strong understanding and practice of Islamic values, cultivated through tahfidz up to 30 juz and daily worship habits.

Character Building

Character developed through the 5S culture (Smile, Greet, Salute, Polite, Courteous) and five core values every day.

Academic Excellence

A quality, creative, and critical learning culture — with national achievements in science olympiads and debates.

English Development

English proficiency trained to international standards through immersion and the Cambridge English program.
