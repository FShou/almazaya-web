Our Programs

From early childhood through secondary — Accredited A with the Merdeka Curriculum grounded in Islamic education.

Preschool · Modern Montessori International

MMI Al Mazaya Preschool

In partnership with MMI London, Al Mazaya brings authentic Montessori education to Kalimantan for the first time. The curriculum nurtures independence, curiosity, and a love of learning from the earliest ages.

  • Mum & Me (ages 6–24 months) — early stimulation with parents
  • Playgroup (ages 2–3) — structured Montessori play
  • Montessori Kinderworld (ages 3–6) — full curriculum programme
  • Hands-on learning with authentic Montessori materials

JHS · Accredited A

Junior High School

Future foundations start in grade 7: the Foundation Program at the start of each year — 2 months of culture-building matriculation (5S, memorization, core subjects) followed by an intensive English Quarantine, then the annual overnight English Camp.

  • 3-month Foundation Program: culture matriculation + English Quarantine
  • English Camp — tutors from Kampung Inggris Pare, Kediri
  • Step-by-step tahfidz with per-semester targets
  • Science Camp — real-world projects (lobster farming)
  • Early science olympiad coaching (4 national medals in 2024)

SHS · Accredited A

Senior High School

Maximum preparation for top state universities. Our graduates have been accepted at UI, ULM, ITS, and ITB — including general & dental medicine and UI Computer Science.

  • Super Camp — project-based learning in Math, Physics, Chemistry
  • Students Club & Enrichment — talent coaching + individual mentoring
  • Intensive UTBK & university-invitation-track coaching
  • Advanced tahfidz & 30-juz hafizh recognition
  • Cambridge English certification for world-standard graduation
