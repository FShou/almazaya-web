MMI Al Mazaya Preschool

In partnership with MMI Singapore, Al Mazaya brings authentic Montessori education to Kalimantan for the first time. The curriculum nurtures independence, curiosity, and a love of learning from the earliest ages.

  • Mum & Me (ages 6–24 months) — early stimulation with parents
  • Playgroup (ages 2–3) — structured Montessori play
  • Montessori Kinderworld (ages 3–6) — full curriculum programme
  • Hands-on learning with authentic Montessori materials
