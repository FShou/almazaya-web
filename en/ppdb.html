Documents

General Requirements

  • Copy of birth certificate / family card (KK)
  • Copies of diploma & SKHUN (or latest report card for early grades)
  • Color photos 3×4 cm (3 pieces)
  • Copy of vaccination card / health certificate
  • Completed parents' data form & interview

* Final requirements are informed by the admission committee upon registration.

Common Questions

Admission FAQ

Must applicants already be able to read the Qur'an?

No. Students are guided from the basics through tahsin and gradual tahfidz programs with targets suited to each student's ability.

Does the school accept non-Muslim students?

As an Islamic school with daily worship guidance, please contact the admission committee via WhatsApp for the latest policy information.

How does the tuition system work?

Detailed registration and education fees per level can be asked directly to the committee via WhatsApp +62 811-5051-123 or by visiting the school.

Is there an achievement pathway?

Students with academic/non-academic achievements (olympiads, tahfidz, etc.) may receive special provisions. Submit the portfolio during registration.
