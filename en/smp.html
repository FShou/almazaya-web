Junior High School

Future foundations start in grade 7: the Foundation Program at the start of each year — 2 months of culture-building matriculation (5S, memorization, core subjects) followed by an intensive English Quarantine, then the annual overnight English Camp.

  • 3-month Foundation Program: culture matriculation + English Quarantine
  • English Camp — tutors from Kampung Inggris Pare, Kediri
  • Step-by-step tahfidz with per-semester targets
  • Science Camp — real-world projects (lobster farming)
  • Early science olympiad coaching (4 national medals in 2024)
