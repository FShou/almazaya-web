Foundation

Al Mazaya Pelita Asia Foundation

Al Mazaya Islamic School operates under the Al Mazaya Pelita Asia Foundation, founded by H. Taufik Hidayat. The foundation manages primary and secondary education in Banjarmasin with a strong focus on Islamic character and academic excellence.

Education at Al Mazaya rests on three pillars: academics aligned with Kurikulum Merdeka, religious & character education practiced daily, and technology mastery — plus daily English and Arabic immersion.

Our Journey

History of Al Mazaya

Key milestones from our founding to the present day.

2015

Foundation Established

Al Mazaya Pelita Asia Foundation was established to deliver quality Islamic education in Banjarmasin.

2016

Junior High Opens

Al Mazaya Islamic School Junior High welcomes its first cohort, expanding into secondary education.

2019

Certified Cambridge English School

MoU with Cambridge Assessment English made Al Mazaya the first Certified Cambridge English School in Banjarmasin.

2020

English Camp & Immersion Programs

Annual English Camp and international language immersion programs become a regular part of student life.

2022

Graduation & Tahfidz Recognition

Five students honoured for memorising 30 juz of the Qur'an, showcasing the school's commitment to Quranic study.

2024

Achievements & Expansion

Outstanding results in national olympiads and debates, plus expansion of international partnerships and overseas programmes.

Direction & Goals

Vision & Mission

Vision

To shape an excellent, noble Islamic generation — intellectual, faithful in personality, equipped with life skills, and devoted in worship.

Mission

  • Deliver Islamic-based education and character building
  • Develop students' academic & non-academic potential optimally
  • Equip students with technology and international language skills
  • Build daily worship habits and noble character
Values & Culture

Adab Before Knowledge

Six core values that guide every student, teacher, and member of the school community.

Sincerity

Intention aligned with Allah in every step of learning and doing.

Integrity

Trustworthy in every responsibility, big and small.

Noble Character

Polite, respectful to teachers and parents — adab before knowledge.

Love of the Qur'an

Daily recitation and memorisation as part of school routine.

Independence

Self-discipline and responsibility — learning to stand on one's own.

Academic Excellence
