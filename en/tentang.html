About Al Mazaya

Al Mazaya Islamic School

Al Mazaya is an Islamic educational institution committed to shaping a generation that excels in character and intellect.

Operating under the Al Mazaya Pelita Asia Foundation (YAPA), Al Mazaya offers education with a one-stop education services concept — accompanying students from early childhood through to secondary education.

Throughout our educational process, Al Mazaya integrates four core foundations:

  • Islamic values
  • Character building
  • Academic excellence
  • English language proficiency
    • “Shaping an Islamic generation that excels in character and intellect.”

    Values & Culture

    How We Grow Every Day

    Education is not only about what is learned, but how students behave and grow each day. Five core values shape the character we cultivate.

    Honesty

    Speaking and acting truthfully in every situation.

    Discipline

    Honouring rules and time as a form of respect for the process.

    Responsibility

    Completing tasks diligently and with full awareness.

    Independence

    Being self-reliant and not dependent on others in learning.

    Initiative

    Bold to start and take the first step proactively.

    School Culture 5S Smile · Greet · Salute · Polite · Courteous 5R Sort · Set · Shine · Standardize · Sustain

    The 5S & 5R culture fosters a positive, orderly, and mutually respectful learning environment.
